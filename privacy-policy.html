Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

BeBaduy is an interactive cultural learning application that introduces users to the traditional ngahuma farming practice of the Baduy community in Banten, Indonesia. This policy explains how information is handled when you use BeBaduy.

2. Information we collect

BeBaduy does not require users to create an account or log in. The app does not ask users to provide personal information, and it does not request access to the device camera, microphone, or location for its core experience.

3. Device motion

BeBaduy uses device motion sensors to support its interactive 360-degree experience. Motion information is used to respond to device movement within the experience. Before publication, the development team must confirm whether this information is processed only on the device and is not stored or transmitted.

4. Storage and sharing

BeBaduy does not operate an external user account system or backend service. The development team should confirm whether the app or any included SDK stores diagnostic, analytics, or other usage information before this policy is finalized.

5. Third-party services

The development team should review all included frameworks and SDKs and list any third-party services that collect or receive information. Do not publish this policy until that review is complete.

6. Children's privacy

BeBaduy does not knowingly request personal information from users. If you have a question about children's privacy in connection with the app, please contact us.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.
